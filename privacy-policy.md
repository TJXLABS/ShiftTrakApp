# Privacy Policy for ShiftTrak

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** January 7, 2026

---

## Introduction

TJXLABS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ShiftTrak mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

**Our Commitment:** ShiftTrak is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your personal work data stays on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide

When you use ShiftTrak, you may enter:
- Work shift details (dates, times, locations)
- Earnings information (wage rates, pay amounts)
- Employment details (job titles, employer names - optional)
- Tags and notes (custom labels you create)
- Photos (receipts, rosters, or other images you attach to shifts)

**Important:** All this data stays on your device. We never see it or have access to it.

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

**Anonymous Usage Analytics** - We use Google Analytics (Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with ShiftTrak:
- Screens viewed (e.g., Dashboard, Analytics, Settings)
- Features used (e.g., shift creation, exports, PRO features)
- App performance (load times, crashes)
- Device type and country (for optimization)
- Session duration and frequency

**What Analytics Does NOT Collect:**
- ❌ Your name, email, or phone number
- ❌ Your shift data (wages, hours, employer details)
- ❌ Any personally identifiable information (PII)
- ❌ Your photos or notes

**Crash and Diagnostic Data** - Anonymous error reports sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs and improve app stability:
- Device model, Android version (only collected during crashes)
- Anonymous crash logs and stack traces

---

## How We Store Your Information

### Local Storage

- **All your personal data** is stored locally on your device using SQLite database
- Your shift data, earnings, photos, and settings **never leave your device**
- We do not have access to your personal information
- We do not store your data on any cloud servers
- We do not have servers or databases that collect user data

### Third-Party Services

We use the following Google Firebase services:

**Firebase Analytics:**
- Collects anonymous usage data (screens viewed, features used)
- Uses anonymous device IDs (not linked to your identity)
- IP addresses are automatically anonymized
- Helps us understand which features are popular and improve the app
- Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

**Firebase Crashlytics:**
- Collects anonymous crash logs when the app encounters errors
- Does not include your shift data, earnings, or personal information
- Helps us identify and fix bugs
- Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

### Access and Export
- View all your data within the app
- Export your data as PDF or CSV files (PRO feature)
- Your data remains on your device

### Data Deletion
- Delete individual shifts from the shift details screen
- Clear all app data by uninstalling the app or using Android Settings > Apps > ShiftTrak > Clear Data
- Once deleted, data cannot be recovered (we don't have backups - it's only on your device)

### Analytics
- Analytics are anonymous and cannot be linked to you personally
- You cannot opt-out while using the app, as analytics help us improve ShiftTrak for everyone
- Analytics data contains no personal information

### Crash Reporting
- Crash reporting is enabled by default to help improve the app
- You cannot opt-out while using the app, but crash reports contain no personal data
- Helps us fix bugs that affect all users

## International Data Transfers

**Analytics Data:** Anonymous usage data is processed by Google (Firebase) and may be stored on Google's global infrastructure (primarily US data centers). This data is anonymous and cannot be linked to you personally.

**Your Personal Data:** Never leaves your device, so it is never transferred anywhere.

---

## Data Retention

**Your Personal Data:**
- Stored on your device indefinitely until you delete it
- We never receive or store your personal data

**Anonymous Analytics:**
- Google Analytics retains anonymous usage data for 14 months by default
- After 14 months, old data is automatically deleted

**Crash Reports:**
- Firebase Crashlytics retains crash data for 90 days
- After 90 days, crash data is automatically deleted

## Children's Privacy

ShiftTrak is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

**Note:** Even if a child uses the app, all data stays on their device and we cannot access it.
